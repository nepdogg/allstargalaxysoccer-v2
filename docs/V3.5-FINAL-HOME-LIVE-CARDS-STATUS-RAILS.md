# Allstar Galaxy V3.5 — Final Home Live Cards + Status Rails

## Shared framework change
The two animated energy rails in the universal status bar now ping-pong continuously:
- top rail: left → right → left
- bottom rail: right → left → right

They remain opposite to each other and use the active section accent color.

## Home-only change
The six Explore the Allstar Galaxy cards are now full-card links with stronger hover/focus treatment and live metrics calculated from `data/master-content.json`.

Current metric sources:
- Galaxy Shuffle: published Full Match + Highlights + Slideshow links plus published award videos.
- Search: published games + players + seasons + playlists + game awards + news.
- Latest Games: published games and their Full Match/Highlights/Slideshow links.
- Game Awards: published award video links.
- Best Of: published playlists assigned to `home-best`.
- Complete Archive: published seasons and playlists.

Because counts are calculated at page load, future Administration publishing only needs to update `master-content.json`; no Home HTML/CSS edits are needed.
